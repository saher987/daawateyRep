Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-09

daawatey ("the app") is a wedding invitation management app. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

Information we collect

When you sign in, we collect only what your chosen sign-in method provides:

  • Google Sign-In: your name, email address, and profile picture, as shared by Google.
  • Email/password sign-in: the email address you provide.

We use Firebase Authentication (a Google Cloud service) to handle sign-in — we never see or store your password.

How we use this information

Your account information is used solely to identify you within the app — for example, to show you wedding invitations addressed to you, or events you manage. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data storage

Account and app data is stored using Google Cloud services (Firebase Authentication, and Google Cloud infrastructure for the app's backend). Data is not transferred to any other third party beyond what's needed to operate these services.

Your choices

You can delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
